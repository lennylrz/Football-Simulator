Five Seasons · six design directions

Same screen, six completely different looks

Each frame shows the redesigned transfer window: the window is now split into four calmer steps, and this is step two, the offers. Real 2015/16 data with Chelsea as the example club. Potential is no longer shown, so spotting the next star is down to your ball knowledge.

02

Super Sunday

The late-2000s to 2015 TV-football feel: glossy gradients, angled score-bug panels, condensed italic capitals and a light sweep across cards. Inspired by the era's broadcast graphics, with no real logos, typefaces or branding.
