Four ways to make 2015 feel like 2015

All four borrow the language of the console football games of 2012 to 2015: floodlit stadium blur behind every menu, big tiles, a career inbox, slanted tabs and a button-prompt bar along the bottom. Here the prompts are keyboard keys, because this is a laptop game. They are built to look deliberately of their time, without any real logos, typefaces or branding.

Live Tiles

The tile-grid hub of 2013 and 2014. Every tile is a live summary (offers, bids, squad, the league) and the selected one is outlined as if picked with a controller. Light-weight type, big numbers, a dimmed floodlit stadium behind.

B

Career Inbox

Career mode in 2012 and 2013 was run from an inbox: the chief scout, the board and other clubs all wrote to you, and a calendar strip showed the days to the next event. Offers arrive as a scout report and bids as emails. The most "you are the manager" of the four.

Team Management

The squad screen of that era: a tilted 3D pitch with name plates on the left, a list on the right, and orange highlights. Offers are shown as transfer targets, and the selected one appears on the pitch where he would play, with his position fit.
